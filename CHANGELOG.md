# What's new in Skypane

Skypane is being built in a private repository; this is a plain-words list of what each release added. Newest first.
Prefer to browse? See the interactive page: **[What's in Skypane](https://almulder.github.io/Skypane/)**.

## 3.0.40 · Nov 13, 2026
- **New: the Unraid server card** (Unraid 7.2 or newer). It shows the array and pools, parity, CPU, RAM and
  temperature, updates, containers, VMs, the UPS and any drive with a problem. Skypane only reads the server.
- **Mix and match:** every part of the card has its own look (ring, tile, bar, list), order and on / off per card size,
  with five presets to start from. Tap the card for every disk, pool, container and the parity history.
- The page's settings in the layout builder are now in tabs too: Page · Look · Dock · Grid.

## 3.0.39 · Nov 13, 2026
- **Builder settings in tabs:** a card's settings are split into Shows · Style · Actions · When · Place, with a dot on
  any tab that has something set.
- **Searchable dropdowns:** every device picker is one dropdown with its search inside.
- 3D printers can now be added in the guided setup (Bambu Lab only for now).
- Save a single card or a whole page as a template.

## 3.0.38 · Nov 13, 2026
- **People & pets drawings:** people with outfits, hair, glasses, skin tones and mobility aids, who show home or away
  from a presence sensor; dogs and cats; reptile and pet habitats that show the temperature and turn amber or red
  outside the animal's range.
- **Chicken coops** with the auto door, heat lamp and waterer.

## 3.0.37 · Nov 13, 2026
- Device drawings bring their own scenery, so they never get a circle or square behind them.
- "No background" now really means none in every theme.

## 3.0.36 · Nov 13, 2026
- **About 1,500 new device drawings** in 16 groups: lights, fans, appliances, kitchen, laundry, media, heating and
  cooling, energy, security, garage, pool, plants, trees and more. Each one shows its device live (on / off, brightness
  and color, fan speed, shade position, door open or closed, a plant's moisture).
- **Pick a drawing per device** for the whole house, or per card, from a new Drawings tab with search.
- Per drawing: its colors, menus (model, style) and scenery on or off. Plant and tree builders let you pick the plant,
  the pot and the flower color.

## 3.0.35 · Nov 12, 2026
- 3D printer cards: the printer's camera can go full screen, and spools always show in a compact row.

## 3.0.34 · Nov 12, 2026
- A big 3D printer card grows its drawing, numbers and spools with its space; small cards show each spool's material.
- Smoother animations on older wall tablets.

## 3.0.33 · Nov 12, 2026
- 3D printer drawings stay smooth while printing: only the numbers change.
- Tap the printer to see its camera, and tap again for the drawing.

## 3.0.32 · Nov 12, 2026
- A 3D printer card can show the printer's **camera**: live video on the X1, H2 and H3, snapshots on the P1 and A1.

## 3.0.31 · Nov 12, 2026
- **Bambu Lab 3D printers:** a card per printer, drawn as its own model, with the toolhead moving and the part growing
  as it prints. Progress, time left, layer, temperatures, and every AMS with each spool's color, material and humidity.
- A **3D printers** card shows all of them at a glance.
- Panels can alert when a print finishes, fails or runs out of filament.
- Read only and local: nothing goes through the cloud, and nothing on the printer changes.

## 3.0.30 · Nov 12, 2026
- **Sonos groups** on the speaker card: each speaker's volume and mute, plus a group volume.
- A spinning **record player** shows when there's no album art.

## 3.0.29 · Nov 12, 2026
- Speaker card: Play and Pause respond at once, and the progress bar runs smoothly.

## 3.0.28 · Nov 12, 2026
- **Internet speed test** on the Internet card (UniFi): a live gauge for ping, download and upload, then the result
  and the last five tests. Can ask for the panel PIN first.
- The speaker card's background takes on the album cover's color.

## 3.0.27 · Nov 12, 2026
- **Real album art, glow and progress** on the speaker card, plus shuffle, repeat and crossfade, from Sonos Panel.
- Holiday calendars: pick the kinds of observances to show, and a **Popular only** switch for the well-known days.

## 3.0.26 · Nov 11, 2026
- **Holiday calendars** split into Federal, State, Observances and Religious, each with its own switch; the same
  holiday never shows twice.
- **Speaker card:** two new styles (Cover and Strip), big round touch buttons and a progress row.
- **Timers & countdowns** get their own page in the Manager; one button on the panel starts, pauses and resumes.

## 3.0.23 – 3.0.25 · Nov 11, 2026
- Fixes and polish: steadier bins, readable see-through network cards, solid pop-ups, speaker artwork back, and a
  choice of how soon personal views go back to everyone.

## 3.0.22 · Nov 11, 2026
- Bins: the pickup day is a simple dropdown, with an optional second day for twice-a-week pickups.

## 3.0.21 · Nov 11, 2026
- **Themes that draw their own cards,** and five premium themes: Lucent, Reach, Residence, Alpine Lake and Pine Mist.
- **Personal views:** the panel puts the person at it first, with their rooms, calendar, to-dos and favorites.
- Set the color of the panel's own notification lights when there's no alert.

## 3.0.20 · Nov 10, 2026
- Gave the Home page its own theme? Saving asks whether to use it for the whole panel.

## 3.0.18 · Nov 10, 2026
- **Kids theme characters come alive:** all 60 blink, wag, wave or flap, each on its own routine, and jump when tapped.

## 3.0.15 – 3.0.17, 3.0.19 · Nov 10, 2026
- Fixes and polish: kids theme characters now show on every page and on older tablets, and several themes look right
  on older wall tablets.

## 3.0.14 · Nov 10, 2026
- **Nineteen new themes:** Aluminum, Atelier, Clear and Remote, plus fifteen kids themes (Dinosaur, Ocean, Space
  explorer, Princess, Pixel quest and more) with characters peeking over the cards.
- **Every theme's background works with any theme.**
- Picking a theme turns on its whole look: card style, background and font.

## 3.0.13 · Nov 9, 2026
- **Five new themes** (Broadsheet, Hololine, Night Drive, Sunday Den, Pebble), each with Light, Dark and Night, its own
  variants and color palettes.
- **Theme templates:** a ready Home page and room page per theme, filled with your own devices.
- **Gates card:** up to four sets of gates in ten animated ways a gate opens.
- **Now playing** card with album art and glow, and a **picture screensaver** with a calm night clock.

## 3.0.12 · Nov 8, 2026
- **A tidier Manager:** live panel status, a snapshot preview of each panel, and a language choice.
- **Look & layout:** themes, backgrounds, rotating pictures, weather effects, a screensaver, and 20 built-in fonts
  (or import your own) per panel, page or card.
- **Your own camera layouts** from 18 templates for 1 to 16 cameras.
- **Holiday calendars** for any country and state, and a birthday cake icon for birthdays.

## 3.0.11 · Nov 8, 2026
- **Garage doors card** with a building look: roof, wall and door colors, windows; 1 to 4 doors.
- **Several UPSes** (UniFi, NUT or SNMP), each with a Connect test; Skypane only reads them.
- New animated bin drawings with lifting lids, and speaker and appliance drawings with real models and colors.
- A better icon picker with categories, search and still or animated icons.

## 3.0.10 · Nov 8, 2026
- **Pair your first tablet** as the last step of the guided setup.
- Simpler panel settings on the tablet; the weather card picks 1, 3, 5 or 7 days, and a tap shows a day's hours.
- UPS over SNMP, and guest Wi-Fi passwords that change at a time you pick.

## 3.0.9 · Nov 7, 2026
- **A guided setup** from the very first sign-in: home location, preferences, connecting Hubitat, the message broker,
  the weather and optional extras (people, calendars, guest Wi-Fi, cameras, UPS).
- Every connection has a **Test** and says in plain words what's wrong.
- **US weather warnings** from the National Weather Service, with no key needed.

## 3.0.6 – 3.0.8 · Nov 6, 2026
- **The Skypane Helper app** for Hubitat takes over from Maker API: one app, set up in a few guided steps.
- Fixes and polish.

## 3.0.5 · Nov 6, 2026
- **One blank card for everything:** devices, fans, text, values and pictures are pieces you place anywhere.
- **Undo and redo everywhere,** and a trash with Restore, so nothing in use can be deleted by accident.
- New cards: notifications, UPS / battery backup, doorbell and a UniFi activity feed.
- **Lists & notes** you can fill from Hubitat rules ("add to list: Shopping, milk").

## 3.0.4 · Nov 5, 2026
- **One Devices card** for lights, switches, readings, buttons, locks, fans and scenes, as a list, tiles, freeform or
  one big item.
- **Bins / Trash** with animated bins, real schedules, holiday shifts and a reminder.
- **Countdowns & timers** that can flash lights and speak on house speakers when time is up.
- **Nine UniFi network cards:** internet lines, a live network map, clients, PoE and more.
- House-wide units and date formats, and a 14-day forecast.

## 3.0.3 · Nov 4, 2026
- Weather fixes, cards that keep their text size as you resize them, and a soft PIN pad sound.

## 3.0.2 · Nov 2, 2026
- **Panels connect with a code** and only work on the tablet you approved.
- Integrations show their real status, and a press and hold on a light opens brightness, warmth and color.
- Calendar event icons matched on words.

## 3.0.1 · Nov 2, 2026
- Fixes and polish.

## 3.0.0 · Nov 1, 2026
- **The first release for testers.** Wall panels on any tablet with rooms, lights, locks, the thermostat, garage doors,
  cameras, calendars, the weather and an animated sky that follows the real one.
- **The Manager** on a computer or phone, with a drag-and-drop layout builder, accounts for the family, and health
  checks.
- Built in: a message broker, camera video and automatic backups. Secrets never reach a browser.
- Send feedback right from the Manager.
